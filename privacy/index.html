Privacy Policy

Updated November 10, 2026

Best Belize Adventures (bestbelizeadventures.com) is a guide to reef snorkeling, cave tubing, Maya ruins and other adventures in Belize, with an inquiry form for travelers who want help planning a tour. This policy explains what we collect and how it's used.

Tour inquiries

If you use the inquiry form, we receive the details you enter, such as your name, email, the adventure you're interested in and your message. The form is delivered through a lead server run by our publisher, Eye To Ad Media (clients.eyetoad.com). We use it to answer your question, and if you ask about a specific tour we may pass your request to a local operator who runs it so they can reply. We don't sell your information or add you to a mailing list.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4. It sets cookies to measure how many people visit, which pages they read, their device type and approximate region, so we can see which adventures readers care about.

Google AdSense and advertising cookies

This site uses Google AdSense to show ads. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the internet.

You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, or opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising at www.aboutads.info. Read how Google uses information from sites that use its services. Visitors in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland are asked for consent before personalized ads are shown.

Your choices

You can block or delete cookies in your browser at any time. To ask about or delete an inquiry you sent, email (shown when the page loads) or call 1-800-481-8638.

Children and changes

The site isn't meant for children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect their information. If this policy changes, the date above changes too.
